About SBS Who we are

A specialist SME consulting and development firm rooted in Somaliland.

Smart Business Solutions (SBS) is a dedicated SME development and consulting firm in Somaliland. We provide advisory, entrepreneurship training and digitalization through a strategic partnership with Saafi Systems.

Vision

Building a competitive, digital SME sector.

Our vision is to become the leading SME development and consulting firm in Somaliland, helping to create a competitive and digitally enabled private sector that can thrive regionally and globally.

  • Regional outlook. We support Somaliland to build a resilient, opportunity-rich SME ecosystem that attracts investment and talent.
  • Digital future. We guide enterprises from paper-based processes to robust, integrated digital systems that make day-to-day management easier.
Mission

Equipping entrepreneurs with skills, systems & support.

Our mission is to equip entrepreneurs and SMEs with the skills, systems and support they need to start, manage and grow sustainable businesses that create jobs and long-term impact.

  • Deliver hands-on entrepreneurship training based on the ILO SIYB programme.
  • Offer tailored consulting in strategy, operations and financial planning.
  • Provide digital tools that simplify management for schools, clinics, SMEs and construction firms.

Ready to strengthen your SME or programme?

Our Approach How we work

A clear, step-by-step model that combines diagnostics, advisory support and digital tools.

Every engagement blends diagnostics, capability building and technology – all anchored in the realities of the Somaliland market and your specific context.

  • 1
    Diagnostic & SME assessment We begin by understanding your business model, customers, finances and operations. This highlights key constraints, risks and opportunities for growth.
  • 2
    Tailored advisory & training package We design a customised mix of consulting, coaching and ILO SIYB-based training – using practical, local examples and tools you can apply immediately.
  • 3
    Digital systems implementation Where relevant, we deploy school, clinic, ERP or construction systems to digitize your processes, improve data quality and strengthen decision-making.
  • 4
    Long-term follow-up & support We stay on board as a trusted partner – reviewing progress, refining strategies and supporting your team so improvements are sustained over time.
