About SBS

Smart Business Solutions (SBS) is Somaliland’s specialist partner for SME consulting, entrepreneurship training and digital transformation.

Based in Hargeisa, SBS blends global best-practice tools – including the International Labour Organization’s Start and Improve Your Business (SIYB) model – with deep local insight to deliver practical, results-oriented support.

From first idea to full-scale expansion, we help entrepreneurs, schools, health centres and construction companies build sustainable, profitable and future-ready organisations.

Together, this combination of global standards and local experience ensures every client receives targeted, practical support that turns strategy into measurable progress.

Quick facts

  • Head office in Hargeisa, serving all regions of Somaliland.
  • 100+ organisations supported through advisory, training and systems.
  • Official implementer of ILO’s SIYB entrepreneurship programmes.
  • Digital solutions delivered in partnership with Saafi Systems.

Vision

To become Somaliland’s leading SME development and consulting firm, enabling a competitive, digitally empowered private sector that creates jobs and opportunities.

Mission

To equip SMEs and entrepreneurs with the skills, systems and tools needed to start, manage and grow strong, resilient businesses.
